## Problem Statement:-
Student often lack immediate ,centralized access to theur academic performance metrics - such as marks ,and attendance and struggle to understand how their attendance directly affect their overall academic standing .Futhermore, traditional portals rarely offer comparative insights against class averages ,personalized advice to help student improve . There is a need for a lightweight , interactive console portal that validates students identity , predicts final grade , and provides actionable feedback based on attendance to marks correlation.
## Scope of the Project:-:
1. Single common password authentication coupled with case-insensitive student name lookup across pre-stored records.
2. Displaying individual student attendance percentages and exam marks.
3. Calculating real-time class averages for both marks and attendance across all registered students.
4. Automated calculation of a predicted final score using a weighted formula.
5. Alogrithm analysis of attendance versus academic performance to identify behavioral trends.
## Target Users
Students:- enrolled individuals seeking to review their academic progress . check predicted grades , view class benchmarks and receive feedback on their marks and attendance.
Educators and Academic Advisors :- Instructors looking for a concise , algorithmic , model to demonstrate how attendance weighting and class average impact individual student performance.
## High Level Features
1. Authentication and name search:
Secured credentials validation via common password and case-insensitive name matching loop to lock to locate student records acuurately
2. Class average calculator:
Iterative computation of class-wide average marks and attendance percentages.
3. Weighted Grade Predictor:
Calculator predicted final scores.
Multi-tier conditional evaluation for standard letter grades.
4. Attendance vs Performance Correlation Engine
Evaluates combinations of attendance and marks to output targeted insights on study or attendance habits.
5. Comprehensive Academic Report and Advice Generator:
Summary printout displaying student metrices alongside rounded class average
Comprehensive feedback comparing student marks .
